Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about our property management services in Uganda.

We perform background checks including national ID verification, proof of employment/income, rental history references, and in-person interviews to ensure trustworthy tenants for your property.

We provide regular photo/video inspection reports, transparent monthly financial breakdowns, and direct transfers to your designated bank account or mobile money line.

Our fees are competitive and calculated as a fixed percentage of monthly rent collected. There are no hidden charges or surprise maintenance markups—we only earn when your property earns.

We maintain a vetted network of licensed plumbers, electricians, and technicians. Routine repairs are cleared with the owner first, while 24/7 emergencies are addressed immediately to protect structural integrity.

We enforce legally binding tenancy agreements with clear payment schedules. In default scenarios, we manage formal notices and dispute resolutions strictly within Ugandan tenancy law frameworks.
